Questions for the manufacturer
Dear sir,

Please see our responses in line below. We have answered each point from the manufacturing and production perspective. Where a requirement is project-specific or dependent on the final construction, we have noted this accordingly.
Questions for the Manufacturer
1. Do you hold BS 4790 and EN 13501 fire certification? Can you supply the certificates, and are they issued per batch, per product or per material?
Answer:
Our fire certification availability depends on the specific rug construction, fibre composition and backing system. We can provide the relevant certificates where applicable. Fire performance certification is generally related to the tested construction/material specification, rather than being automatically issued for every individual production batch. The applicable certification and testing basis would need to be confirmed against the final specification of the rug.
2. Is the certificate for the finished rug, with face yarn, latex, backing and binding tested together, or for the face yarn only?
Answer:
This depends on the particular test certificate. Fire performance can vary according to the complete rug construction, including face fibre, backing and other components. Where a finished-rug test is required, the test should be carried out on the complete construction specified for the project. We would need to confirm the exact test report available for the proposed construction.
3. Which EN 13501-1 class does it achieve?
Answer:
The EN 13501-1 classification is construction-specific and cannot be confirmed independently of the final rug specification. We can confirm the applicable classification once the fibre, backing and construction have been finalized and matched against the relevant test report.
4. Do you hold a fire rating for wall hangings, which hang vertically? For example BS 5867 Part 2, or a non-flooring EN 13501 class.
Answer:
Vertical wall-hanging applications are different from flooring applications and require the appropriate test standard for the intended use. This would need to be assessed separately based on the final wall-hanging construction and backing system. We can discuss the required specification and testing route for this application.
5. Is the fire performance inherent to the wool, or is it a treatment? If treated, does it survive professional cleaning, and does it need reapplying?
Answer:
Wool has naturally good flame-resistant characteristics due to its fibre properties. However, the final fire performance of a rug depends on the complete construction. Where any additional fire-retardant treatment is specified, its cleaning durability and whether re-treatment is required would depend on the particular treatment system. We would confirm this against the final specification before production.
6. Whose name appears on the certificate, and can one be issued in our name or against our order number?
Answer:
The name appearing on a test certificate depends on the testing arrangement and the party commissioning the test. Where a project-specific test is required, we can discuss having the relevant documentation linked to the customer's project/order reference, subject to the laboratory's requirements.
7. Do you hold Oeko-Tex Standard 100, REACH or azo-dye-free certification?
Answer:
Our compliance with REACH requirements and azo-dye restrictions can be addressed according to the yarn and dyeing materials used for the project. OEKO-TEX certification is product/material-specific and would need to be confirmed against the exact yarn and construction selected. We can provide the relevant supporting documentation where available.
8. What is the exact fibre specification: wool grade, origin and blend percentages? What changes between grades?
Answer:
We can offer different wool qualities and specifications depending on the required appearance, handle, durability and budget. The exact wool grade, origin and blend percentage will be confirmed in the final product specification and approved before production. Changes between grades can affect the rug's softness, lustre, resilience, appearance, dye response and overall cost.
9. What is the backing made of, including the latex compound and the secondary backing? Is there a non-latex option?
Answer:
The backing system depends on the rug construction and intended application. Our standard constructions can include latex and a secondary backing where required. Alternative backing systems, including non-latex options, can be considered depending on the project requirements. We would confirm the exact backing specification before production.
10. Is the backing suitable for use over underfloor heating?
Answer:
This depends on the final backing system and the manufacturer's requirements for the underfloor-heating installation. We would need to confirm the exact construction before giving approval for use over underfloor heating. The installation should also follow the underfloor-heating manufacturer's temperature and ventilation recommendations.
11. What is the maximum pile height? Can you do carving, multi-level pile, or mixed loop and cut pile? Does any of that change the price?
Answer:
Yes, we can produce different pile heights and constructions, including carving, multi-level pile and combinations of loop and cut pile. The practical maximum depends on the construction, design and required density. Additional carving, multiple pile levels and more complex constructions can affect both production time and price, so these would be quoted according to the final CAD and specification.
12. Can you make shaped pieces such as circles, curves and cut-outs around hearths and columns? Is there a surcharge?
Answer:
Yes, we can produce shaped rugs including circles, curved shapes and cut-outs around architectural features such as columns or hearths. Any additional cost would depend on the complexity and size of the shape and would be confirmed at quotation stage.
13. What edge finishes do you offer, which is standard, and can the binding be colour-matched?
Answer:
We can offer different edge finishes depending on the construction, including standard overlocking/binding and other specified finishes. The standard finish will depend on the rug construction. Binding can generally be colour-matched to the rug or selected to complement the design, subject to yarn availability.
14. What is the finished weight per sqm at 6mm, 12mm and 20mm pile?
Answer:
Finished weight varies according to pile height, density, yarn specification, construction and backing. Therefore, there is no single standard weight for each pile height. We can provide an estimated finished weight once the exact construction and density have been finalized.
15. Do you hold any colour fastness or shedding test data?
Answer:
Colour fastness depends on the fibre, dye class, colour and dyeing process. Shedding is also construction- and fibre-dependent. Relevant testing can be arranged where required, and we can provide available test data for the specified yarn/construction.
16. What do you warrant, and for how long?
Answer:
Our warranty covers manufacturing and workmanship defects subject to the agreed product specification and normal use. The exact warranty period and conditions would be confirmed in the commercial quotation/order confirmation for the project.
17. What exactly does the warranty cover: pile loss, latex backing coming away, colour fade, edge binding failure, shedding beyond normal settling?
Answer:
Warranty coverage is generally related to manufacturing and workmanship defects. Normal characteristics of handmade rugs, including normal shedding, minor tonal variation, and changes associated with normal use and maintenance, are not considered manufacturing defects. Specific coverage for backing failure, edge failure or abnormal pile loss would be assessed against the agreed construction and warranty terms.
18. If a piece fails in year three, who remakes it and who pays?
Answer:
If a confirmed manufacturing defect falls within the agreed warranty period and warranty terms, the appropriate remedy would be discussed based on the nature of the issue. Where replacement or remake is required due to a manufacturing defect, responsibility would be determined according to the agreed warranty conditions. Damage resulting from misuse, incorrect cleaning, installation or normal wear would not be covered.
19. Is the warranty different for wall hangings, which are under constant tension rather than underfoot?
Answer:
Yes, wall hangings are a different application and should be assessed separately. Constant tension, hanging method, support structure and installation can affect the product differently from normal floor use. We would therefore recommend agreeing a separate specification and warranty conditions for wall-mounted pieces.
20. What are your care and cleaning instructions, and which cleaning methods void the warranty?
Answer:
Regular low-to-medium suction vacuuming using a suitable attachment is recommended. Aggressive rotating brushes or hard brush attachments should be avoided where they may catch or disturb the pile. Professional cleaning should be carried out by an experienced rug-cleaning specialist using a method suitable for the specific fibre and construction. Incorrect cleaning, excessive moisture, harsh chemicals, aggressive brushing or unsuitable machinery may invalidate warranty coverage.
21. What is the tolerance on an ARS 1400 colour match?
Answer:
Colour matching is subject to the characteristics of the selected fibre, dyeing method and the ARS 1400 reference. Handmade and hand-dyed rugs can have natural tonal variation, and an exact visual match cannot always be guaranteed. The acceptable tolerance should be agreed against an approved physical strike-off/sample rather than relying only on a digital or printed reference.
22. Is there dye-lot variance between runs?
Answer:
Yes. Some dye-lot variation is possible, particularly with natural fibres and hand-dyeing processes. We make every effort to maintain consistency by retaining the approved colour reference and dye recipe, but a small degree of natural variation can occur between separate dye lots.
23. If a piece comes back outside your stated tolerance, what is your remedy?
Answer:
If an approved colour specification or agreed tolerance is not achieved, we would review the piece against the approved physical reference and determine the appropriate corrective action. Depending on the situation, this may include colour correction, re-dyeing where technically possible, or remake/replacement subject to the agreed commercial terms.
24. Can you produce a strike-off or sample piece, and at what cost and lead time?
Answer:
Yes, we can produce strike-offs and sample pieces. Cost and lead time depend on the size, number of colours, yarn specification and construction. These would be quoted separately based on the project requirements.
25. How long do you keep the design file and yarn recipe? Could you remake an identical piece in year three or five? Can yarn be reserved for a future matching order?
Answer:
We retain production records, including relevant CAD/design information and production specifications, subject to our internal record-retention practices. We can use these records to support a future remake; however, an absolutely identical result cannot always be guaranteed after several years because yarn availability, dye lots and raw materials can change. Where long-term colour matching is important, we recommend retaining an approved physical reference and, where commercially practical, reserving sufficient yarn.
26. Is 15m x 10m a single piece or seamed? If seamed, how visible is the join, and at what size does seaming start?
Answer:
The feasibility of producing a 15m x 10m piece as one continuous rug depends on the selected construction, loom/tufting limitations, finishing equipment and handling requirements. Where a seam is necessary, its position and visibility would be considered during CAD and production planning. The maximum single-piece size would be confirmed before order confirmation.
27. What are realistic lead times at 6 sqm, 20 sqm and 50 sqm, and what extends them?
Answer:
Lead times depend on the construction, design complexity, yarn availability, dyeing requirements, sampling/approval process and current production schedule. We would provide a project-specific lead time at quotation stage for 6 sqm, 20 sqm and 50 sqm rather than giving a generic timeframe. Delays can occur where there are changes to CAD, colour approvals, special yarn requirements or complex finishing.
28. What is your monthly capacity, and what happens when two large orders land in the same week?
Answer:
Capacity depends on the construction and the mix of production running at the time. Production is scheduled according to confirmed orders and required delivery dates. If multiple large orders are received simultaneously, we allocate production capacity according to the agreed schedule and communicate realistic delivery dates before accepting the order.
29. Do you run one facility or more than one? If a run fails part way through, what is the fallback?
Answer:
Production planning and backup arrangements depend on the specific construction and process involved. Where a production issue occurs, we assess whether the work can be corrected or restarted within the same production route. Any alternative production arrangement would be discussed and agreed before proceeding to ensure that construction and quality remain consistent.
30. What does a wall hanging weigh at the maximum of around 18 sqm?
Answer:
The final weight depends on pile height, density, yarn specification and backing. For an 18 sqm wall hanging, we would calculate the expected finished weight once the final construction has been approved. This is important not only for shipping but also for determining the appropriate hanging/support system. Roughly weighs around 45 to 50 kgs approx.
31. Do you supply or fit a hanging system, such as a rod pocket, sleeve or Velcro batten? What do you recommend at that size?
Answer:
We can provide a rod pocket as part of the rug finishing. The actual hanging system, including the rod, battens, brackets, wall fixing and installation, must be arranged by the customer at the installation site.
For a large wall hanging of approximately 18 sqm, we recommend that the customer’s installer/contractor select and install the appropriate support system based on the rug's final weight and the wall structure.
32. Is the backing reinforced against sagging under constant tension, and is there a maximum unsupported width?
Answer:
For large wall hangings, the backing and support system should be designed specifically to prevent excessive sagging and distortion. The appropriate reinforcement depends on the final size, weight and hanging method. A maximum unsupported width should therefore be determined during the engineering and installation stage rather than applied as a standard figure to all constructions.
33. What is included in the price per sqm and what is not: design, sampling, binding, backing, packing?
Answer:
Pricing is normally based on the agreed final specification. Depending on the quotation, the price may include the rug construction, standard backing and standard finishing. Design/CAD development, sampling/strike-offs, special binding, special backing, additional finishing, packing and freight can be quoted separately where applicable. We will clearly identify inclusions and exclusions in the final quotation.
34. What is your minimum order, both per piece and per order?
Answer:
Minimum order requirements depend on the construction, yarn, dyeing requirements and production method. For custom handmade rugs, we can generally consider individual pieces, subject to the specific project and commercial viability. Any minimum requirements for special yarns or dyeing would be advised at quotation stage.
35. Which Incoterms do you quote on? Who is the importer of record, and who pays duty?
Answer:
We can quote on the basis of the agreed Incoterm, such as EXW, FOB or CIF/CIP, depending on the destination and shipping arrangement. The importer of record and responsibility for import duties/taxes depend on the selected Incoterm and should be clearly stated in the commercial invoice and order documentation.
36. How are large pieces packed, and what is the maximum roll length before it has to go as freight? How would a 15m x 10m piece ship?
Answer:
Large rugs are normally carefully rolled, protected with suitable wrapping and packed according to the size, weight and shipping method. The practical packing and shipping method for a 15m x 10m rug would be determined after confirming the final construction, finished weight and transport requirements. Oversized pieces may require dedicated freight rather than standard parcel shipment.
37. Who insures goods in transit, and who makes the claim if a piece arrives damaged?
Answer:
This depends on the agreed Incoterm and shipping arrangement. The party responsible for transit insurance would be identified in the quotation/order confirmation. Any damage should be documented immediately upon receipt, including photographs of the packaging and rug, and reported within the carrier/insurance claim period.
38. What are your payment and deposit terms, and what is owed if an order is cancelled after cutting has started?
Answer:
Payment and deposit terms are agreed at order confirmation and depend on the project and customer relationship. As these are custom-made rugs, cancellation after production or cutting has commenced would normally be subject to recovery of costs already incurred, including materials, dyeing, labour and production. The exact cancellation terms would be confirmed in the quotation/order confirmation.
39. Will you confirm in writing that you gain no rights in the design, and will not reproduce, resell or sample it for anyone else?
Answer:
Yes. For customer-supplied designs, we understand that the design remains the customer's intellectual property. We can confirm in writing that we will not reproduce, resell or knowingly use the customer's proprietary design for another client, subject to the agreed contractual terms.
40. Will you sign an NDA and a white-label agreement covering both of the above?
Answer:
Yes, we are open to signing a mutually agreed NDA and white-label/confidentiality agreement covering design confidentiality, non-reproduction and the agreed commercial relationship. The final documents would be reviewed and agreed by both parties before production begins.
