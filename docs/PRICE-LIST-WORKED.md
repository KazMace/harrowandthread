# Worked price list (from mill email, 2026-08-30)

**Internal only. Never publish these numbers.**

All figures are **£ per m²** (Kaiser's instruction 2026-08-30: per-m² only, no fixed-size rug totals).

The flat per-rug costs are spread over 6 m², the 2 × 3 m size the FedEx quote covers.
A larger rug lowers Ship and Cert per m²; larger rugs also switch to air/sea freight.

- Shipping (FedEx, 2 × 3 m): Flatweave £270 → £45/m²; Hand tufted / knotted £445 → £74/m².
  Sumak/jute assumed the flatweave rate — **not confirmed by the mill**.
- Certificate of origin: £40 per rug → £7/m² (exempts duty).
- Duty: £0 on everything except hand-knotted 100% pure silk — mill is still checking. Marked ⚠.
- VAT: per Kaiser 2026-08-30, applied ONLY to 100% pure silk rows (20% of buy + ship).
  Bamboo/Tencel silk are man-made fibres, treated as not-silk.
  **Unverified flag (raised once, not to be re-raised):** UK import VAT normally applies to
  all imports; the mill's "zero rate except silk" line was about duty. Accountant to confirm.

Formulas (per m²): Total cost = buy + ship + cert + VAT.
**Sell = buy × 3** (Kaiser 2026-08-30: markup off the actual buy price only, not off the
loaded cost). Profit = sell − total cost.
